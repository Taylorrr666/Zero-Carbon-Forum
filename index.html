KEY INSIGHTS

Decarbonisation is not one-size-fits-all

Sector matters

The most material emissions sources vary significantly between restaurants, hotels, pubs and experiential leisure businesses.

Control matters

Leased, managed and franchised operating models create different levels of control over energy, data and implementation.

Procurement matters

For some businesses, food, beverage and supply-chain emissions may represent a greater opportunity than building energy alone.

Simplicity matters

Complex sustainability data requirements can prevent businesses from translating climate ambition into practical action.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Building a more personalised decarbonisation journey

01. Smarter Questionnaire

Tailor questions according to sector, material topics and operational control.

02. Three-Tier Benchmarking

Combine peer comparison, hybrid benchmarking and action-based performance assessment.

03. AI-Assisted Data Extraction

Reduce manual reporting by extracting relevant information from sustainability reports, utility bills and internal documents.
